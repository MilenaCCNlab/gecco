3 · Disclosures

This is a descriptive, protocol-matched comparison — not a held-out claim. The held-out results (with train/test separation) are in report.html and are not numerically comparable to this page (different data span).
  • Per-participant composition selects modules on the participant's own data, exactly as individual GeCCo selects program structure — the two are information-matched; neither is held-out.
  • 8 of the 30 participants are library-extraction seeds and 5 were composition-validation participants (shared program only); the individual bars are equally in-sample (each program evolved on its own participant).
  • The library is the 19-module leak-free subset. Individual GeCCo programs keep their original mechanisms, including within-trial outcome leakage where present (e.g. participant 3) — that inflates the individual bar in its own favor.
  • Stored baseline/group/individual values come from the original gecco runs (analysis/rlwm/*_bics.csv); composed fits replicate that protocol (blocks < 5, missed trials dropped, per-participant fits, seeded 10-restart L-BFGS-B).

4 · Reproducibility

  • Shared composed fits: analysis/rlwm/plot_composed_comparison.py → composed_plotspan_bics.csv.
  • Per-participant compositions: greedy forward search per participant over module_inventory_leakfree.json on the same filtered data → perpid_plotspan_results.json.
  • Frozen shared program: composed_model.txt; library provenance and extraction fidelity: see report.html sections 4–5.
