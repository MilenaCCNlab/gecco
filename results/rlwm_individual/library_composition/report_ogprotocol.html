4 · Cross-checks & disclosures

  • 14 of 15 canonical refits differ from baseline_bic by |Δ|>5; mean Δ -14.2 BIC (refits mostly better — our 10-restart protocol finds better optima). Under the full-trial span the same comparison was off by +145–260: the column was computed on this blocks<5 span.
  • The shared winner was selected under the full-trial span (validation pids 10–19) and only evaluated here — a disclosed protocol mismatch that, if anything, handicaps it.
  • Per-participant composition selects modules on the participant's own data, exactly as individual GeCCo selects structure — information-matched, not a held-out shared-program claim. The shared-composed and group/canonical rows ARE held-out claims.
  • Library is the leak-free 19-module subset; individual programs keep their original mechanisms, leaks included, inflating the individual bar in its own favor.
  • Small samples (15 + 7); greedy search; see report.html for the full-span results, extraction fidelity, and the complete decision log.
