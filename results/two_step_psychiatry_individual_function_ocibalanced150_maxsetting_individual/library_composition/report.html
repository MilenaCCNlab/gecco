Generated 2026-07-19. Gemini generator gemini-3.1-pro-preview. All results on the 50 held-out test participants; the composed winners were frozen on the 50 validation participants before test data was touched.

1 · What was run

150 Gillan-2016 participants, OCI-tertile-stratified (cuts 9/18), split 50 train / 50 validation / 50 test (shuffled, no index–OCI confound). Individual GeCCo fit all 150; group GeCCo used 5 in-context + 50 validation. A 27-module library was extracted from the 50 train programs and composed two ways: bare-bone (greedy from backbone) and hybrid-base (exhaustive over modules added to the Daw-hybrid module set).
